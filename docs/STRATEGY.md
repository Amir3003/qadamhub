# QADAM Hub: strategy context

Source: owner brief, October 2026. Use it for every text, deck, landing page and new feature. Writing rules: no em dashes, no emojis, say "AI & Machine Learning" instead of dated academic labels such as "Computer Science".

## 1. Current product
- B2B SaaS and AI career office for schools, colleges, IT hubs and universities. 149 USD per month per cohort.
- Problem: in 2022 the founder missed more than 20 hackathons because links got lost in chats and deadlines passed. Staff paste links into chats and track students in spreadsheets; students lose opportunities.
- Solution: one feed of verified grants, internships and hackathons (deadline confirmed by a quote on the organizer's page), student notifications in Telegram (and KakaoTalk for South Korea), CV packaging (CV Benchmark Lab), and one school dashboard showing who is preparing and who applied.

## 2. Positioning for Y Combinator (target batch Spring or Summer 2027, framed on the owner's reading of YC Requests for Startups)
Move away from "a link aggregator" towards three theses:
- "The 15 dollar an hour expert": private admissions and career consultants cost thousands; QADAM gives an ordinary school curator AI tools to work at the level of an elite career agency for hundreds of students at 149 USD per month.
- "Plaid for benefits and scholarships": one verified student profile, confirmed by the school, to apply for scholarships, government and international grants and internships in one click, without fraud or re-filling forms.
- "LLMs that write well": CV Lab and essay help that package applications in natural language for a specific benchmark, without template filler.

## 3. New direction: Lifelong Student Portfolio and National School Ranking (insight from the Parasat business club and Karaganda investors)
- Problem: Kazakhstan employers lack talent and cannot judge hard plus soft skills from a CV.
- Through-profile from school to university graduation, four blocks: technical achievements (hackathons, projects, internships); academic metrics (UNT, GPA, IELTS, JLPT N2 and similar); character and discipline (verified volunteering, sports); verification by the institution's curator.
- School Leaderboard: student points add up to a public national ranking of schools and universities by practical activity. Schools subscribe (149 USD per month) to climb it and prove results to parents and akimats.
- Employers (plants, corporations, business clubs) publish real R&D cases in the radar and hire verified graduates through their portfolio.

## 4. Investor requirements (MOST Holding CEO Pavel Koktyshev, venture funds)
- Pre-revenue metric: month-over-month growth in CustDev interviews, pilots launched with schools and signed letters of intent (LOI), not only revenue.
- International from day one: Kazakhstan, then South Korea (KakaoTalk), Japan, Central Asia and global.
- Strong founding team with clear roles: product and full-stack, AI and backend for the radar core, strong English (IELTS 7.5) for international networking.

## Honesty guardrails (status in the code, October 2026)
- Live: radar with quoted deadlines, CV gap check, 236 skill quizzes, Telegram and Discord bots, invite-only student access, early staff workspace (cohorts, invite codes, statuses, CSV).
- Not live yet: KakaoTalk channel (blocked on a Korean phone number), one-click applications, verified profile, portfolio, school ranking, employer cases, essay generation. Present these as roadmap.
- No customers, pilots or LOIs yet: use real numbers or placeholders, never invented traction.
